LIFEPAD is a privacy-first, all-in-one Android app that unifies knowledge
management, CBT-enhanced journaling, and personal finance tracking — a
spyware-free alternative to multiple productivity apps. Everything stays
on your device.

Features:
* Notepad: Markdown notes, wikilinks, checklists, folders, full-text search
* Journal: mood tracking, template entries (gratitude, reflection,
savoring, check-in, food journal), streaks and statistics
* CBT tools: thought-journal restructuring wizard, exposure journal with
SUDS ratings, 12 thinking traps, GAD-7 and PHQ-9 assessments with trends
* Finance: transaction tracking, budgets, custom categories, accounts
* Unified graph view connecting notes, entries, and transactions
* Export to JSON, CSV, or Markdown
